Recording and conducting the score for Princess Cruises’ Phantom Bridge

One pair of hands,
from cut to score.

Evan Monheit is a Los Angeles–based producer, editor and composer with over twenty years across film, television, music and immersive entertainment. His work bridges high-profile media projects with deeply human narratives.

At Hearst Media Production Group he served as Supervising Producer and Lead Editor on Oddly Satisfying Science for Curiosity Stream, directed segments for NBC’s Earth Odyssey and ABC’s Oh Baby!, and was Supervising Post Producer and Lead Editor on the Emmy-nominated Play It Forward with the Harlem Globetrotters, now in its third season on NBC.

His scoring work spans feature films, trailers, immersive media and modern dance — the full score for the horror feature The Chosen, ghostwriting for Academy Award–winner John Ottman on Hammer Films’ The Resident, and trailer campaigns for The Dark Knight Rises, Poltergeist, Versace, Orphan and Cowboys & Aliens.

In 2025 he returned to his roots with Bloomers: The Lost Season — fourteen new episodes of the award-winning series, completed frame by frame as a one-man post team in honour of his late friend, creator Matt Palazzolo.
